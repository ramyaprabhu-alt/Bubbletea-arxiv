\paragraph{MoE routing and serving optimizations.}
Load imbalance in sparse \ac{MoE} execution is well known and has been addressed through routing objectives and capacity constraints~\cite{switch,gshard}, expert replication and placement, and
serving-time mechanisms such as \textsc{EPLB}~\cite{eplb}, \textsc{LPLB}~\cite{lplb}, and \textsc{Waterfill}~\cite{waterfill}.
\textsc{DeepEP}~\cite{deepep}, \textsc{pplx}-kernels~\cite{pplxkernels}, and lower-precision execution further reduce communication and expert compute.
\sys is complementary: it uses the residual idle capacity that remains after these optimizations are applied.

\paragraph{Inference--fine-tuning colocation and GPU multiplexing.}
\flexllm~\cite{flexllm}, \llmstation~\cite{llmstation},
\harli~\cite{harli}, and \deltaserve~\cite{deltaserve} colocate latency-critical inference with best-effort fine-tuning by exploiting prefill and decode underutilization or serving-level headroom alongside idle periods. 
\sys instead targets per-GPU slack that appears \emph{inside} expert-parallel prefill, requiring admission from the current router state rather than serving-level signals.
More broadly, GPU- and LLM-multiplexing systems use temporal sharing, operator interleaving, spatial partitioning, or model-residency management~\cite{gandiva,antman,orion,nvidiamps,nvidiamig,
muxserve,qlm,serverlessllm,aegaeon,prism}.
These systems allocate resources across workloads or models, whereas \sys reclaims per-GPU slack inside an ongoing \ac{MoE} forward pass.
\textsc{ConServe}~\cite{conserve} co-serves online and offline inference of one model and argues against lending idle cycles to training because of process- or container-switch costs; \sys avoids that cost by training on the served weights in place.

\paragraph{Multi-tenant \ac{LoRA} serving.}
\textsc{Punica}~\cite{punica}, \textsc{S-LoRA}~\cite{slora}, and \textsc{dLoRA}~\cite{dlora}
improve inference across many already-trained \ac{LoRA} adapters sharing one base model.
They optimize adapter \emph{serving}, whereas \sys uses otherwise-idle serving capacity to \emph{train} adapters and produces ordinary checkpoints compatible with such downstream systems.
