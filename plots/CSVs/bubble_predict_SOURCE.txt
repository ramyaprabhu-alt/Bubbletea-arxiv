What: absolute error (ms) of the deployed slack predictor vs a static
per-layer profile, pooled over 193,920 (prefill layer x rank) samples.
  deployed  budget_r = 6 us x (max_q tiles_q - tiles_r),
            tiles = sum over the rank's experts of ceil(tokens_e / 128)
            (bubbletea/bubble_predict.py; no fitted parameters)
  static    per-layer mean bubble of the first half of the run, all ranks
  measured  bubble_r = all-reduce window_r - shortest window across ranks
  errors on the second half of each run (prefill steps split by time)
Setup: Qwen3-30B-A3B, BF16, 4x A100, TP=EP=4, vLLM serving without EPLB,
five loads 4k@8, 8k@4, 8k@2, 12k@2, 2k@1 (tokens@QPS), 400 arXiv requests,
32 output tokens; bubble probe (VLLM_BT_BUBBLE_PROBE), 2026-10-01.
